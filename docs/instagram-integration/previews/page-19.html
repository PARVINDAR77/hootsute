17 Production Deployment & Meta App Review

Transitioning from development sandbox to public live production requires navigating Meta's App Review process, completing Business Verification, and hardening production infrastructure.

Meta App Review Submission Blueprint

1. Screencast Walkthrough Requirements

• High-definition (1080p) video with clear voiceover.

• Demonstrate user clicking "Connect Instagram".

• Show Meta login dialog with requested permissions.

• Show post composition, image upload, and scheduling.

• Verify post appearing live on the Instagram app.

2. Demo Credentials & Reviewer Access

• Provide active test user credentials in review notes.

• Pre-populate workspace with sample media assets.

• Ensure staging environment is publicly reachable.

• Keep reviewer accounts active until review concludes.
